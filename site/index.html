Who we are

We are the map-makers of industry

IndusBrain was born from a simple observation: every industrial project — a solar farm, a hospital, a brewery — is really a network of dozens of companies that must connect in the right order. That network is invisible until something goes wrong. We made it visible.

  • Depth over noise. Our moat is jurisdiction depth — real regulators, real laws, real payment sequences, verified and dated.
  • Built for the deciders. Enterprise and government-grade: audit trails, citations, access control and traceable sources.
  • Global ambition, local precision. Deep-mapped ecosystems starting in Nigeria, expanding country by country — while AI covers everywhere else today.

What drives us

Trillions are lost every year to projects that stall because someone didn't know a permit, a payment or a partner existed. We believe every industry deserves a complete map before the first dollar moves — and that the same brain that maps ecosystems can imagine new ones.


That's why IndusBrain is both an atlas and an inventor: it documents every industry ecosystem on earth, and it composes new ideas from them — with no limit to what it can create.
